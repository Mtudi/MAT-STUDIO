# Motion Playground — Reference Desk

Research references for the isolated MATUDI Official Portfolio motion experiments.

## Primary inspiration and implementation references

1. **Codrops Creative Hub** — current experimental web motion, typography, transitions, WebGL and scroll-driven examples.  
   https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/all/

2. **Codrops — Kinetic Typography Page Transition** — background lettering moves into the foreground to reveal a new content level.  
   https://tympanus.net/codrops/2021/09/29/kinetic-typography-page-transition/

3. **Codrops — Inspiration for Text Block Transitions** — word-level transitions for storytelling and content changes.  
   https://tympanus.net/codrops/2023/07/13/inspiration-for-text-block-transitions/

4. **Codrops — On-Scroll Expanding Image Animation within Typography** — imagery expands inside typographic forms while scrolling.  
   https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/04/02/on-scroll-expanding-image-animation-within-typography/

5. **Codrops — Text Character Animations with GSAP** — 19 character-by-character examples, including WebGL experiments.  
   https://github.com/codrops/TextCharacterAnimations

6. **GSAP SplitText documentation** — character, word and line splitting; includes responsive splitting and accessibility considerations.  
   https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/SplitText/

7. **Codrops page-transition collection** — examples of clip-path, grid, image, scroll and typography transitions.  
   https://tympanus.net/codrops/hub/tag/page-transition/

## Research notes

- The live playground currently contains ten original CSS/JavaScript prototypes inspired by these techniques; they are not copies of the linked demos.
- No GSAP dependency is loaded in the playground. The prototypes are deliberately lightweight and self-contained.
- These references are inspiration and implementation research, not an assertion that their original effects are already integrated into MATUDI Portfolio.
- Before production use, evaluate motion intensity, keyboard access, mobile layout, reduced-motion behavior, and actual device performance.
