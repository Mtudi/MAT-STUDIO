# MATUDI DEUTSCH — A1 Curriculum

This is an original beginner learning path informed by CEFR A1 communication goals and the four skill areas used in Goethe-Institut A1 examination practice. It is not official Goethe content and does not claim to complete the full A1 syllabus.

## Course map

| Unit | Theme | Language focus | Skill practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greetings & introductions | Personal pronouns, sein, heißen, country/language phrases | Listen to introductions, read a profile, write and say a self-introduction |
| 2 | Family & personal details | der/die/das, mein/meine, haben | Family dialogue, short profile reading, personal description |
| 3 | Daily life & time | Present tense, verb-second word order, um + time | Daily schedule listening and reading, routine description |
| 4 | Food & shopping | möchten, polite requests, basic accusative | Café listening, menu reading, ordering practice |
| 5 | Home & city | es gibt, simple location and direction words | Directions listening, apartment description and reading |
| 6 | Work & study | können, müssen, infinitive at sentence end | Student/work profile, responsibilities and self-description |
| 7 | Hobbies & travel | gern, mögen, mit + transport | Travel dialogue, schedule reading, hobby and travel plans |
| 8 | Health & everyday communication | sein, brauchen, polite questions with Sie | Asking for help, requesting repetition, short message writing |

## Daily 60-minute routine

1. 10 minutes — review vocabulary that is due.
2. 15 minutes — learn a unit's vocabulary with articles and examples.
3. 15 minutes — study the grammar note and create original sentences.
4. 10 minutes — listen to the dialogue and answer a comprehension question.
5. 10 minutes — repeat the speaking model and write a short response.

The session checklist is self-reported and is not a timer or proof of study time. Session and streak data are stored locally.

## Learning design

- Nouns are taught with their articles (der, die, das).
- Flashcards use active recall and a simple interval schedule.
- A due-only queue keeps review focused; "Again" moves a card to the end of the current queue.
- Listening uses browser text-to-speech with the `de-DE` locale. Voice availability and quality vary by device.
- Reading has short original texts with comprehension questions.
- Writing prompts provide a model answer for comparison; they are not automatically graded.
- Speaking practice asks learners to listen and repeat. No microphone recording or speech-recognition grading is claimed.
- Progress remains in browser local storage; there is no cross-device sync.
